# AID Commercial License

## Overview

This document outlines the commercial licensing terms for the AID programming language and its associated tools for entities generating revenue from products or services built with AID.

## Who Needs a Commercial License?

A Commercial License is required if you:

- Build and sell a product or service that uses AID as a core component
- Offer AID-based functionality as a managed or hosted service
- Embed the AID compiler, runtime, or Cortex engine in a commercial product
- Use AID to build SaaS, PaaS, or API products offered to paying customers

## Who Does NOT Need a Commercial License?

- Individual developers using AID for personal or learning purposes
- Open source projects (with proper attribution)
- Companies using AID for internal tools that are not sold externally
- Educational institutions and research
- Non-profit organizations

## Revenue Share Model

Commercial licensees agree to the following revenue share based on annual gross revenue generated from AID-based products:

| Annual Revenue from AID Products | Revenue Share |
|----------------------------------|---------------|
| Under $100,000 USD               | 0% (free)     |
| $100,000 - $1,000,000 USD        | 0.1%          |
| $1,000,000 - $10,000,000 USD     | 0.5%          |
| Over $10,000,000 USD             | 1.0%          |

**Notes:**
- Revenue is calculated on the gross revenue directly attributable to products built with AID
- Revenue share is assessed annually
- First $100,000 in annual revenue is always free (startup-friendly)
- Revenue share applies only to the revenue tier (marginal, not flat)

## Attribution

All commercial products must include the following attribution in documentation, about pages, or similar prominent locations:
